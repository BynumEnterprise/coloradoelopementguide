Every list of Colorado elopement spots names the same five places and none of them tells you whether you need a permit. This one does. Below are twenty specific spots, at least two in each of our eight regions, chosen for a real view, reasonable access and a permit situation you can actually manage. Each has a short note, its permit status as of October 2026, and a link to the page on this site that explains the rules in full.

Two habits will serve you everywhere. Go at sunrise, when the light is soft and the crowds are asleep. And keep it small, because almost every public site in Colorado gets stricter as your group gets bigger.

## Estes Park and Rocky Mountain National Park

### Sprague Lake
A flat, accessible loop around a lake that mirrors Hallett Peak, the easiest RMNP site for guests who cannot hike. Bear Lake Road is jammed by mid morning in summer, so this is a sunrise site.
**Permit:** RMNP special use permit required, $300; 15 people in summer, 30 in winter. [RMNP permit guide](/resources/rocky-mountain-national-park-wedding-permit)

### 3M Curve
A Trail Ridge Road pullout looking across Moraine Park to Longs Peak, and the park's signature winter site because the road stays open to here. Expect wind.
**Permit:** RMNP permit required; 15 people and three vehicles. [RMNP permit guide](/resources/rocky-mountain-national-park-wedding-permit)

### Lily Lake
Outside the main entrance stations south of town, with the postcard view of Longs Peak and three permitted spots of different sizes. Good for grandparents.
**Permit:** RMNP permit required; 10, 20 or 30 people depending on the spot. [Estes Park guide](/guides/estes-park)

## Boulder

### Chautauqua lawn
The open meadow below the Flatirons at 900 Baseline Road, ten minutes from downtown. Paid parking on summer weekends, with a free shuttle from satellite lots.
**Permit:** none for groups under 50; a city large group lawn permit for 50 to 100. [Boulder guide](/guides/boulder)

### Sunrise Amphitheater, Flagstaff Mountain
A circular stone amphitheater built in 1933 with the city and plains below. Early morning blocks are the bargain and the best light.
**Permit:** reservation through the City of Boulder, May through October. [Boulder guide](/guides/boulder) and [listing](/vendor/sunrise-amphitheater)

### Brainard Lake
An hour up the Peak to Peak Highway, a lake at 10,300 feet with the Indian Peaks reflected in it. Timed entry reservations are required in summer and fall.
**Permit:** none for a small group with no props on national forest land; wilderness rules limit group size beyond the lake. [National forest guide](/resources/national-forest-and-blm-elopements)

## Denver and the foothills

### Red Rocks Park
The famous monoliths are forty minutes from the airport, and the park around the amphitheatre is public land owned by Denver. On concert days the park clears early.
**Permit:** Denver special occasion permit for a private gathering, or book the venue's own wedding spaces. [Red Rocks and foothills guide](/resources/red-rocks-and-denver-foothills-ceremony-sites)

### Mount Falcon
Stone ruins of an old mansion and views both ways from a Jefferson County park above Morrison. A mile of easy trail from the west trailhead.
**Permit:** Jeffco trail groups are currently capped at 14 people; larger gatherings need a county permit. [Red Rocks and foothills guide](/resources/red-rocks-and-denver-foothills-ceremony-sites)

### Guanella Pass
A paved road to 11,670 feet south of Georgetown with Mount Bierstadt across the willows. The lot fills with hikers by 7 a.m. on summer weekends.
**Permit:** none for a tiny ceremony with no props; dispersed national forest. [Denver and foothills guide](/guides/denver-foothills)

## Colorado Springs and Pikes Peak

### Garden of the Gods
Red sandstone fins with Pikes Peak behind them and six designated wedding areas, from Jaycee Plaza to High Point. Mild enough for a January ceremony.
**Permit:** none; free and first come, no decorations, posted capacities of 25 or 50. [Garden of the Gods guide](/resources/garden-of-the-gods-elopement)

### Cheyenne Mountain State Park
A state park on the south edge of the city with an amphitheater facing the mountain and room for a real guest list.
**Permit:** special activity agreement with Colorado Parks and Wildlife, plus a vehicle pass. [State parks guide](/resources/colorado-state-parks-elopements)

## Summit County

### Sapphire Point Overlook
Dillon Reservoir and two mountain ranges from a short, easy trail, under two hours from Denver. The site stays open to the public during your reservation.
**Permit:** Recreation.gov reservation, three hour blocks, up to 30 people, no chairs. [Sapphire Point guide](/resources/sapphire-point-overlook-elopement)

### Loveland Pass
The Continental Divide at 11,990 feet on US 6, with a pullout and a short ridge walk. Wind and weather change by the minute.
**Permit:** nothing to book for a tiny ceremony with no props. [National forest guide](/resources/national-forest-and-blm-elopements)

## Vail and Aspen

### Maroon Bells Amphitheater
The most photographed peaks in Colorado, with a small stone amphitheater above the crowded lakeshore. The reservation includes vehicle passes up a road otherwise closed to cars by day.
**Permit:** Recreation.gov reservation, opened a year ahead. [Maroon Bells guide](/resources/maroon-bells-elopement-permit)

### Independence Pass
Highway 82 crests the Continental Divide at 12,095 feet east of Aspen, with tundra a few steps from the lot. The pass closes from roughly early November to late May.
**Permit:** nothing to book for a tiny ceremony with no props; call the ranger district for anything more. [Vail and Aspen guide](/guides/vail-aspen)

### Shrine Ridge, Vail Pass
A moderate four mile round trip through July wildflowers to a ridge facing Mount of the Holy Cross. A hike in ceremony for a fit group.
**Permit:** none for a small group; dispersed national forest. [Vail and Aspen guide](/guides/vail-aspen)

## Telluride and the San Juans

### Alta Lakes
Alpine lakes at 11,200 feet below Wilson Peak, reached by a rough road in a high clearance vehicle from about mid June to October. No reservations and no services.
**Permit:** none for a small group with no props; dispersed national forest. [Telluride guide](/guides/telluride-san-juans)

### Ridgway State Park
An overlook by the visitor center with the San Juans across the reservoir and room for up to 100 guests. The practical choice when grandparents are coming.
**Permit:** special activity agreement with the park. [State parks guide](/resources/colorado-state-parks-elopements)

## Northern Colorado

### Horsetooth Rock
A steady two and a half mile climb to the notched summit above Fort Collins, with the reservoir below and the plains beyond. Sunrise is the reward.
**Permit:** Larimer County daily entrance permit per vehicle; small ceremonies go through a simple county permit. [Northern Colorado guide](/guides/northern-colorado)

### Red Feather Lakes
Small lakes in the pines at 8,300 feet, an hour northwest of Fort Collins and far from any crowd. Cool in July, golden in late September.
**Permit:** none for a small group with no props on national forest land. [National forest guide](/resources/national-forest-and-blm-elopements)

## How to choose between them

Start with your guest count. Two to ten people can go almost anywhere on this list. Fifteen to thirty narrows you to the RMNP sites that allow it, Sapphire Point, Maroon Bells, the larger Garden of the Gods areas and state parks. Beyond thirty, you are choosing among state parks, the Chautauqua lawn and private venues; our [micro wedding venues list](/venues/micro-wedding-venues-colorado) covers the venues.

Then check the season. The high passes and Alta Lakes are summer and early fall only; our [fall](/resources/colorado-fall-elopement) and [winter](/resources/winter-elopement-colorado) guides cover the shoulder months, and [Crested Butte](/resources/crested-butte-elopement) and [Ouray](/resources/ouray-elopement) have their own pages. Garden of the Gods, Red Rocks, Chautauqua and the Front Range sites work most of the year.

Finally, line up the paperwork. The license comes from any county clerk; see [how to get married in Colorado](/resources/how-to-get-married-in-colorado) for the order of steps, and [what a Colorado elopement costs](/resources/colorado-elopement-cost) for the budget. When you know the spot, find a [photographer who knows it](/category/photographers).
