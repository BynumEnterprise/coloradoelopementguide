Our original [Colorado elopement cost page](/resources/colorado-elopement-cost) gives honest ranges for every budget line based on what vendors advertise. This page is the 2027 edition and it does two things differently. Every government fee was rechecked against the issuing office in November 2026. And the vendor lines are no longer ranges we eyeballed: they are the listed starting prices on Colorado Elopement Guide, November 2026, 108 vendors, shown as the 25th percentile, the median and the 75th percentile for each category. A starting price is the lowest package a vendor publishes, so expect to land above it once you add hours, travel or a second shooter. Nothing here is a quote.

## Fixed fees for 2027

These are the only numbers on the page that are not estimates. The agency sets them and the agency can change them, so the link in the sources list wins if it disagrees.

| Item | 2027 fee | Set by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marriage license | $30 | Every Colorado county clerk | Verified at Larimer and Denver; Denver adds a 2.5 percent card fee. Use within 35 days, return within 63 or pay $20 plus $5 a day up to $50 |
| Certified copy | $1.25 | County clerk | Larimer and Denver price; some counties bundle copies into a higher counter price, see our [Summit County guide](/resources/summit-county-marriage-license) |
| RMNP wedding permit | $300 | National Park Service | Non refundable, paid after the draft permit arrives; plus a $30 one day or $35 seven day vehicle pass per car |
| Maroon Bells Amphitheatre | $200 a day | White River National Forest on Recreation.gov | 50 people, five vehicle passes included, extra guests ride the shuttle; reservations open one year ahead |
| Hermit Park pavilion | $500 | Larimer County | 150 people, plus $10 per vehicle to enter |
| State park ceremony | $4 to $17 daily pass per vehicle or person | Colorado Parks and Wildlife | Overlooks and amphitheaters may also need a special activity agreement and a facility fee set by the park |
| National forest, under 75 people, no props | $0 | US Forest Service | Props, commercial use or a wilderness location trigger a permit that takes six months to a year |
| Sapphire Point Overlook | About $120 to $142 | Dillon Ranger District on Recreation.gov | Three hour block, 30 people; see our [Sapphire Point page](/resources/sapphire-point-overlook-elopement) |

The license is the one fee that does not move with your plan. The permit line runs from zero to $500 depending on the land, and the choice of land tends to drive the rest of the budget: a national forest pullout usually means no chairs, no arch and a short guest list, while a pavilion or private venue brings a rental fee and the things a rental lets you do.

Our [2027 Colorado Elopement Report](/resources/colorado-elopement-report-2027), which hand checked 150 vendor websites in October 2026, found a higher photographer median of $4,000 among the 24 that print a price plainly; the difference is the sample, and both point to $3,000 to $4,500 as the realistic middle.

**Photographer.** The largest line for almost every couple. The middle half of listed starting prices runs $2,000 to $4,500. A starting price is usually the shortest coverage a photographer offers, often two to four hours. Full day coverage, a second shooter, video and travel to the San Juans all sit above the 75th percentile. Browse [photographers](/category/photographers) and compare what each starting package includes before you compare the number.

**Officiant.** $0 if you solemnize your own marriage, which Colorado allows under C.R.S. 14-2-109 with no witnesses. Listed officiant starting prices cluster tightly around $440, with the middle half between $381 and $531. A custom written ceremony with a rehearsal and dawn travel runs higher. See [officiants](/category/officiants) and the [self solemnization page](/resources/self-solemnization-colorado).

**Florals.** The median listed starting price is $775, but the spread is wide: $538 at the low end for a bouquet and boutonniere, $2,225 at the high end where the vendor's lowest package already includes ceremony pieces. Public land rules on installations keep most elopement floral budgets near the low end. See [florists](/category/florists).

**Hair and makeup.** Listed starting prices from $250 to $550 per person, median $350, before early start and travel fees. A 4 a.m. call in Estes Park is normal for a sunrise permit and most artists price it. See [hair and makeup](/category/hair-makeup).

**Planner.** Middle half $1,825 to $3,963, median $2,775. Couples with no guests often skip this line and let the photographer run logistics; couples with a venue, a dinner and a 30 person guest list usually do not. See [planners](/category/planners).

**Venue.** Listed venue starting prices run $2,000 to $5,000 in the middle half, median $3,000, and these are ceremony or small event rentals rather than full wedding packages. YMCA of the Rockies publishes a $1,000 to $6,000 rental range for comparison. Public land is the cheaper route when the rules suit you. See [venues](/category/venues).

## Lodging and travel

Lodging is the line we cannot pull from our database, so these remain estimates carried over from our [original cost page](/resources/colorado-elopement-cost): roughly $150 to $350 a night in Estes Park, Fort Collins and Colorado Springs, $200 to $450 in Breckenridge, Boulder and Golden, and $350 to $700 in Vail, Aspen and Telluride, higher in ski season and late September. Plan on three nights. Travel is a rental car from Denver International, all wheel drive outside July and August, fuel, and the vehicle passes counted above. Non US residents pay an extra $100 per person to enter Rocky Mountain National Park unless they hold an annual or America the Beautiful pass.

## What changed from the 2026 page

The fixed fees did not move: license $30, park permit $300, Maroon Bells $200 a day. What changed is the evidence behind the vendor lines. The 2026 page estimated photographers at $2,500 to $7,000; listed starting prices put the middle half at $2,000 to $4,500, with the reminder that a starting price is a floor. Officiants and hair and makeup came in near the low end of last year's estimates. Florals and venues showed a wider spread than we assumed, which is why this page uses percentiles instead of one range.

## Common questions

### Is the Colorado marriage license really $30 everywhere?

Yes. Larimer County and Denver both publish $30, and the fee is set statewide. What differs by county is how you apply, whether you need an appointment, and whether certified copies are bundled into the counter price. Our [Colorado marriage license guide](/resources/colorado-marriage-license) links each county.

### Which permit is the cheapest for a ceremony with chairs and an arch?

Hermit Park at $500 plus $10 a vehicle, because Larimer County allows your own chairs and event tents there. Rocky Mountain National Park prohibits arches and most decorations at any price, and a national forest ceremony needs a permit as soon as props are involved.

### Why are your photographer numbers lower than other cost guides?

Because they are listed starting prices, the lowest package each vendor publishes, not what couples end up paying. Add hours, a second shooter or travel and the realistic middle is $3,000 to $4,500, which matches what our October 2026 report found on vendor websites.

### Do we pay the $300 park permit fee when we apply?

No. The park reviews the application first, emails a draft permit, and only then asks for the non refundable $300. Vehicle entrance passes are separate and paid at the gate or on Recreation.gov.
