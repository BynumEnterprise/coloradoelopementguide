Plenty of states make you wait a day or three between the license and the vows. Colorado does not. Under C.R.S. 14-2-107 a license is valid from the hour it is issued, and under C.R.S. 14-2-109 the two of you may solemnize the marriage yourselves, so there is no officiant to book and no witness to find. The only real constraint is the clerk's counter: some counties hand you the license while you stand there and some mail it a week later. Pick the right county and you can walk out of the clerk's office at 10 a.m., sign the certificate at a trailhead at noon, and drop it in the mail before dinner.

This page covers the same day route only. The statewide rules, ID list and absentee affidavit are in our [Colorado marriage license guide](/resources/colorado-marriage-license), and how to fill out the certificate as your own officiants is in [self solemnization in Colorado](/resources/self-solemnization-colorado).

## What the law allows

Two statutes do the work. C.R.S. 14-2-107 says the clerk issues licenses only during office hours, that the license shows the exact date and hour of issue, and that it is valid in Colorado for 35 days. There is no language about a waiting period because there is none. C.R.S. 14-2-109 lists who may solemnize a marriage: a judge, a magistrate, a retired judge, a public official with that power, a religious officiant, or "the parties to the marriage." The same section gives you 63 days after the ceremony to get the signed certificate back to the clerk who issued it, with a $20 late fee after that and $5 for each additional day up to $50.

Every clerk we checked restates the same three facts on its own page: no blood test, no waiting period, no witnesses required. Both of you must be 18 or older for the no questions version; the age rules for 16 and 17 year olds involve a court order and are not a same day process.

## Which counties issue the same day

The question is not whether a county issues same day licenses, since every in person license is handed over on the spot. The question is whether you can get in front of a clerk today. Verified on each county's own page in November 2026; hours shift, so click through the morning you go.

The practical pattern: if you are flying into Denver, Golden is 20 minutes from downtown and sits on the way to every mountain region. If you land in Colorado Springs, the El Paso County counter is the only one in the state open on Saturday mornings. In Summit County, a county resident can walk in, so if a friend who lives there is marrying you, the rule is different from the one that applies to visitors.

## What to bring

Each of you needs a current photo ID, which every county reads as a driver's license, state ID, passport or military ID. Birth certificates alone do not count. Bring your Social Security number (there is an affidavit if you do not have one), your parents' names and birthplaces and your mother's maiden name, and, if either of you was married before, the exact date and place the divorce was final or the former spouse died. Denver and El Paso want a certified copy of the divorce decree if the divorce was final within the last 30 days. Most counties ask you to complete the online application before you walk in, and Jefferson, El Paso and Larimer say it speeds the counter. Pay attention to payment: El Paso takes no checks, San Miguel takes no cards.

If one of you cannot be there, the absent partner signs a notarized absentee affidavit and the other appears alone. That still produces a license the same day, but the ceremony itself needs both of you present unless the narrow military proxy rule in 14-2-109(2) applies.

## Where to sign it the same afternoon

Once the clerk hands you the license, the ceremony can be anywhere in Colorado. Three kinds of places work on no notice.

**A trailhead or forest overlook.** On national forest land a group under 75 people with no structures needs no permit; the Forest Service's special use rules kick in at 75 participants or spectators. Loveland Pass, Sapphire Point and the Guanella Pass pullouts are all within two hours of Golden. Our [national forest elopement guide](/resources/national-forest-and-blm-elopements) has the site notes.

**A courthouse lawn or a judge's noon slot.** In Golden, judicial officers perform ceremonies Monday through Friday at noon with no appointment: arrive at the Magistrate Clerk's lobby on the second floor by 11:30 a.m. with the license and $60 in cash. Pick up the license downstairs at 7:30 a.m. and you are married by 12:15 p.m. Our [courthouse wedding guide](/resources/colorado-courthouse-wedding) covers the other courts.

**A town or state park.** A short ceremony for a handful of people in a city park generally needs no permit, but anything with chairs, an arch or more than a few guests usually does, and the rules differ by town. The state park special activity agreement and the national park permit are not same day documents, so save Rocky Mountain National Park and Garden of the Gods for a planned date.

Write the physical place and the time on the certificate, sign in the party lines and the officiant block, and take a photo of both sides before it goes in an envelope.

## Getting the certified copy

The signed certificate goes back to the clerk that issued it within 63 days, by mail or in person; Denver and Boulder also have drop boxes. The clerk records it and returns your original. Order certified copies when you return it: $1.25 each in Denver, Larimer and San Miguel, $1 in El Paso, $3 in Summit (three are included in the $39 fee). Larimer says a certified copy is available immediately when you return the license to the Fort Collins office, and within three days at Loveland or Estes Park. For a name change at Social Security or a passport you need the certified copy, not a photo, so plan on at least two.

## Common questions

### Is there a waiting period to get married in Colorado?

No. C.R.S. 14-2-107 makes the license valid from the exact date and hour it is issued. You can marry minutes after leaving the clerk's office.

### Can we get a Colorado marriage license and get married on a Saturday?

Only in El Paso County, whose North office in Colorado Springs issues licenses Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 1 p.m. Every other clerk we checked is closed weekends, so for a Saturday mountain ceremony pick the license up during the week; it stays valid for 35 days.

### Do we need an officiant or witnesses for a same day wedding?

Neither. C.R.S. 14-2-109 lets the parties solemnize their own marriage, and no county requires witnesses. Sign the officiant block yourselves and leave the witness lines blank or let a friend or child sign them.

### Can out of state couples do this?

Yes. No county has a residency requirement for an in person license. The one catch is Boulder and Denver's virtual process, which mails the license only to a Colorado address, so visitors should use an in person counter.
