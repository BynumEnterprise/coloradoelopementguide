Crested Butte sits at the end of the road in the Elk Mountains, ringed by national forest and some of the best summer wildflower country in Colorado. Most elopements here happen on public land a few minutes from town: an aspen grove on Kebler Pass, a meadow in Washington Gulch, the road toward Gothic. The paperwork is simple. The logistics depend almost entirely on the season and on which roads are open.

## Your marriage license from Gunnison County

Crested Butte is in Gunnison County, and the county clerk's office is down valley in the town of Gunnison, not in Crested Butte. You can use a license from any Colorado county, so if your route runs through Denver or another clerk is easier, that works too. Our [Colorado marriage license guide](/resources/colorado-marriage-license) compares the options.

If you want the Gunnison County license, plan around these rules from the clerk's page and checklist:

- **Appointments only.** Download the checklist, then call (970) 641-2038 to schedule.
- **Where:** the Blackstock Government Center, 221 N. Wisconsin St. in Gunnison. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed noon to 1 p.m. and on holidays.
- **Fee:** $30, payable by cash, check or card. Card payments carry a vendor surcharge of at least $2 or 3.5 percent. Certified copies are $1.25 each.
- **ID:** a current driver's license or state ID, passport, military ID, or an original or certified birth certificate. Colorado IDs marked "Not valid for Federal Identification" are not accepted.
- **Bring the details:** the last four digits of each Social Security number, city and state of birth, a mailing address, both parents' full names (your mother's maiden name) and where they live, and divorce details if either of you has been married.

The county is specific about the appointment itself: only the two of you, no photos or video in the office, and no pets other than service animals. It will not accept a license with a pet paw print on it, though staff can stamp one for you. The license is valid for 35 days anywhere in Colorado, and the signed certificate goes back to Gunnison County within 63 days of the ceremony. You need no witnesses, and you can marry yourselves; see [self solemnization](/resources/self-solemnization-colorado).

## National forest rules

Almost everything around Crested Butte outside town is the Gunnison National Forest, part of the Grand Mesa, Uncompahgre and Gunnison National Forests. The forest's special use page lists weddings as an example of noncommercial group use. It says you need an authorization when:

- your group has 75 or more participants or spectators,
- a fee is charged or income is made from the use, or
- you will occupy, use or build on forest land beyond ordinary visiting.

A couple, a photographer and a few guests standing in a meadow fall well under that line, which is consistent with our [national forest and BLM guide](/resources/national-forest-and-blm-elopements). The forest does not publish rules for individual meadows or trailheads, so if your plan includes chairs, an arch, or a group near the threshold, ask the Gunnison Ranger District, 216 N. Colorado in Gunnison, (970) 641-0471. Applications go to the forest at least 90 days ahead, and processing fees are waived for noncommercial group use. Commercial photography has its own application, so it is fair to ask your photographer how they work on forest land.

## Where couples elope

| Site | Who manages it | What to know |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Kebler Pass aspen groves | National forest along County Road 12 | Dirt road west of town; closed to cars in winter |
| Washington Gulch | National forest | Wildflower meadows; rough road higher up |
| Paradise Divide | National forest | High and exposed; go early for weather |
| Gothic and Snodgrass trail | National forest along Gothic Road | Snodgrass trailhead is just past Mt. Crested Butte |
| Long Lake | Crested Butte Land Trust | Private open space with public access; ask first |
| Woods Walk and Peanut Lake | Crested Butte Land Trust | Reservable ceremony sites |

**Kebler Pass** is the fall favorite: miles of aspen on both sides of the road, with spots to pull off and walk into the trees. **Washington Gulch** and **Paradise Divide** are the wildflower settings, with the upper road getting rougher as it climbs. **Gothic** gives you the old mining town and Gothic Mountain behind it, and the Snodgrass trail starts close enough to town for a short walk to a view.

**Long Lake** is different. The Land Trust manages it as private open space with public access, recreation stays on the near shore, and the northern half of the lake is private property. The Land Trust books ceremonies only at its two venue sites, so check with them at (970) 349-1206 before planning anything at Long Lake.

## Land Trust ceremony sites

If you want a reserved spot with a guarantee nobody else has it, the Crested Butte Land Trust rents two conserved properties. Woods Walk is a short trail through aspens off Kebler Pass Road, with Crested Butte Mountain behind it, and it is open in winter if everyone snowshoes or skis in. Peanut Lake is open in summer and usually melts out around June 15.

The Land Trust's rules: book at least seven days ahead, one event per site per day, up to 100 guests, three hours including setup, no food or alcohol, no amplified sound, no drones, and receptions elsewhere. Parking is limited and cannot be reserved, so you will need a shuttle plan. Booking carries a fee and a refundable cleanup deposit, part of which the Land Trust treats as a donation; check the current terms on its weddings page.

## Roads and seasons

**Kebler Pass** is County Road 12, maintained by Gunnison County Public Works. It closes to cars in winter and reopens in late spring; in 2026 the county opened it on May 21 with a warning about wet, muddy and possibly snowy conditions. Opening and closing notices go up on the county's alert center.

**Gothic Road and Washington Gulch** are dirt roads that hold snow well into spring. Beyond the pavement past Mt. Crested Butte, plan on Gothic Road being closed to cars through winter and the spring mud season. For current conditions on forest roads, the forest tells visitors to call the Gunnison Ranger District.

**Wildflowers** are what Crested Butte is known for. The Crested Butte Wildflower Festival runs ten days each July, July 9 to 18 in 2027, and the festival notes that peak bloom is generally mid July through early August. Book lodging early for those weeks.

**Fall** brings the aspens, usually at their best in late September, though timing moves each year. Our [fall elopement guide](/resources/colorado-fall-elopement) covers when to commit to a date.

**Winter** narrows the options. Crested Butte Mountain Resort has a wedding sales team and on mountain ceremony sites and advises booking at least a year ahead, so ask them what they offer in snow season. In Mt. Crested Butte, the town's Mountain Wedding Garden and pavilion sit in Ted Scheske Town Park next to town hall; the town office is at (970) 349-6632.
