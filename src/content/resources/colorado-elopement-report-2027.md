**For press.** Colorado Elopement Guide is a Denver based directory of Colorado elopement vendors with 455 live listings and a library of permit and license guides checked against official sources. This report uses only data we can show you. Reporters are welcome to the underlying spreadsheet, the vendor price URLs and the raw search volume export; write to us through the [press page](/press). You may quote and chart any figure here with a link to this page.

Three headline findings:

1. **The median published starting price for a Colorado elopement or wedding photographer is $4,000.** That is across 24 vendors whose own websites print a starting price; the middle half sit between $3,000 and $4,775. For the 13 whose figure is specifically an elopement package, the median is $4,200.
2. **Couples shop for elopements in winter, not summer.** US Google searches for "colorado elopement photographer" reached 3,600 a month in February and March 2026, 3.6 times the 1,000 a month recorded in June and July, the months most of those ceremonies actually happen.
3. **Estes Park, the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park, runs on imported vendors.** Of the live photographers, planners, officiants, florists and hair and makeup artists in our directory, 9 are based in the Estes Park area while 102 list it in their service area, about 11 travelling in for every local one. A ceremony permit there costs $300, against $0 at the designated wedding areas of Garden of the Gods.

## What vendors publish as a starting price

Most Colorado elopement vendors do not print prices. In October 2026 we checked 150 photographer, planner, officiant and hair and makeup websites from our directory and recorded a starting price only when the vendor's own page states one plainly. We left out travel fees, albums, headshot and proposal sessions, hourly rates, winter only offers and figures that only describe a top package. Each figure is stored with the exact page it came from. These are published starting prices, not what couples end up paying.

| Category | Vendors with a published price | Median starting price | Middle half (25th to 75th percentile) | Lowest to highest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photographers, all packages | 24 | $4,000 | $3,000 to $4,775 | $995 to $10,000 |
| Photographers, elopement packages only | 13 | $4,200 | $3,000 to $4,700 | $1,750 to $10,000 |
| Photographers, wedding collections and other | 11 | $3,800 | $3,100 to $4,600 | $995 to $6,000 |
| Officiants | 3 | $695 | too few to compare | $550 to $4,300 |

The photographers who market to elopements statewide price in line with the Front Range and Summit County, which fits what couples tell vendors: the photographer is usually the largest line on an elopement budget. ## Permit rules at six popular ceremony sites

Official fees and limits as published by each agency in October 2026. Our detailed guide for each site is linked in the first column.

| Site | Who issues it | Fee | Group limit | When to book |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Rocky Mountain National Park](/resources/rocky-mountain-national-park-wedding-permit) | National Park Service, Special Use Permit | $300 administrative fee, non refundable | 10 to 30 people depending on the site, at 13 designated sites | Applications open one year ahead of the wedding month |
| [Maroon Bells](/resources/maroon-bells-elopement-permit) | White River National Forest via Recreation.gov | $200 per day plus booking fees | 50 people | One year ahead on a rolling basis, 8 a.m. Mountain |
| [Sapphire Point Overlook](/resources/sapphire-point-overlook-elopement) | White River National Forest via Recreation.gov | About $120 to $142 for a three hour block | 30 people; site stays open to the public | Recreation.gov calendar |
| [Garden of the Gods](/resources/garden-of-the-gods-elopement) | City of Colorado Springs | $0 at the six designated wedding areas | 20 to 40 people by area; permit needed over 50 or with chairs, tents or arches | First come, first served; no reservations |
| [Boulder Open Space and Chautauqua](/resources/boulder-elopement-permits) | City of Boulder | Sunrise Amphitheater $100 resident or $150 non resident per dawn hour; Chautauqua lawn free under 50, $25 for 50 to 100 | Varies by site | Sunrise Amphitheater May to October; OSMP ceremonies only at designated sites |
| [Colorado state parks](/resources/colorado-state-parks-elopements) | Colorado Parks and Wildlife, Special Activity Agreement | Daily vehicle pass $10 to $17 depending on the park | Set by each park manager | Some parks ask for 45 to 90 days |

The spread runs from nothing at Garden of the Gods to $300 in the national park, and the two most photographed sites, Rocky Mountain National Park and Maroon Bells, both open their calendars a full year out.

## Marriage license rules by county

Colorado's statewide rules are simple: $30 at any county clerk, no waiting period, no witnesses, no residency requirement, and couples may solemnize their own marriage under C.R.S. 14-2-109. The license must be used within 35 days and returned within 63. What differs by county is how you get it. Verified on each county's own website in October 2026; the [full license guide](/resources/colorado-marriage-license) links every office.

| County | Main office | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Denver | 200 W. 14th Ave., Denver | Appointment only, booked up to 7 days ahead; virtual appointments available with the license mailed |
| Larimer (Estes Park, Fort Collins) | 1601 Brodie Ave., Estes Park | Estes Park by appointment; Fort Collins takes walk ins |
| Boulder | 1750 33rd St., Boulder | Online application, then a video ID check; license mailed, no counter service |
| Jefferson (Golden) | 100 Jefferson County Pkwy., Golden | Walk ins Monday to Thursday; appointments also offered |
| El Paso (Colorado Springs) | 1675 W. Garden of the Gods Rd. | No appointment; cash or card, no checks; Saturday mornings at the North office |
| Summit (Breckenridge) | 208 E. Lincoln Ave., Breckenridge | Non residents book one of four daily slots; $39 including three certified copies |
| Eagle (Vail) | 500 Broadway, Eagle, and El Jebel | Apply online up to 90 days ahead, then book a pickup appointment |
| Pitkin (Aspen) | 530 E. Main St., Aspen | Apply online, then book an appointment; only the couple in the office |
| San Miguel (Telluride) | 305 W. Colorado Ave., Telluride | Walk in Monday to Thursday; cash or check only; closed Fridays |

## Methodology

**Vendor data.** The directory holds 455 live listings across six categories, eight regions and a group of statewide vendors, found through Google Maps business listings and vendor websites and reviewed by hand before release. Counts in this report were taken from our database on October 13, 2026 and include only live listings.

**Prices.** A price was recorded only when a vendor's own website states a starting price for a wedding or elopement package; each is stored with its page URL and the date checked (October 2026). Medians use the standard percentile method on the listed figures. Samples are small and vendors who publish prices may differ from those who do not, so treat these as a guide to published pricing, not an estimate of what couples pay.

**Service areas.** A vendor's home region comes from its business address. Its service area comes from places named on its own website (for example "serving Estes Park and Rocky Mountain National Park") and was compiled in October 2026. A vendor can serve several regions. Statewide vendors have no home region row of their own but are counted in the regions they say they serve.

**Permits and licenses.** Taken from the official agency pages listed below and from our own resource guides, each of which cites its source. Fees change; the agency is the authority.

**Search demand.** Google Ads monthly search volume for the United States, English, retrieved October 13, 2026 through the DataForSEO Keywords Data API. Google rounds these figures into bands, so small differences are not meaningful.

**Corrections.** If anything here is wrong, tell us through the [contact page](/contact) and we will fix it and note the change on this page.
